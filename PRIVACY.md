# Privacy Policy — Zovo Speed

Full policy: https://zovo.one/privacy-policy

## What this extension does with data

- **Settings** (speeds, shortcuts, site rules, learned per-site speeds,
  feature toggles) are stored in `chrome.storage.sync` and synced by your
  browser account. They never leave your browser through this extension.
- **Pro license check** (optional): when you activate a license key, the key
  is sent once to the Zovo license endpoint for verification, and the result
  is cached locally. No other network requests are made.
- **No analytics, no tracking, no ads.** A-B loop points and playback state
  are session-only and never stored or transmitted.

Contact: support@zovo.one
